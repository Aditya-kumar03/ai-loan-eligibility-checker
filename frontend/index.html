          About the Platform & Financial Engine

          Demystifying how credit underwriting, debt ratios, and affordability models evaluate borrowers in India.

          Core Financial Formulations

          1. Equated Monthly Installment (EMI):
          Calculated using the standard reducing-balance amortization formula:
          EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ - 1)
          where P represents principal loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual % ÷ 1200), and n is tenure in months.

          2. Debt-to-Income (DTI) & Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR):
          Indian commercial banks assess whether total monthly obligations exceed allowable thresholds (usually 45%–55% of net monthly income):
          Post-Loan DTI = ((Existing Monthly EMI + Proposed EMI) / Monthly Net Income) × 100

          3. Credit Card Utilization Ratio:
          Measures the portion of revolving credit lines in active use:
          Credit Utilization = (Total Card Balance / Total Sanctioned Limit) × 100
          Bureau scoring models penalize utilization exceeding 30%, viewing it as an indicator of liquidity strain.

          4. Estimated Financial Eligibility Score (0–1000):
          An educational internal benchmark weighting Credit Profile (350 pts), DTI Capacity (250 pts), Employment Stability (150 pts), Credit Line Discipline (150 pts), and Disposable Cash Buffer (100 pts).

          Privacy Policy & Regulatory Disclaimers

          Our data stewardship principles, security architecture, and regulatory boundaries.

          Zero Sensitive PII Architecture

          We strictly do NOT collect, store, or transmit:

          • Full bank account numbers or IFSC codes
          • Credit or debit card numbers, CVVs, or expiry dates
          • Net banking passwords, OTPs, or UPI PINs
          • Aadhaar numbers or government identity credentials

          Audit Sync to Google Sheets

          If configured by the administrator, the backend logs high-level anonymized simulation parameters (age, income bracket, requested amount, calculated score) to a private Google Sheet via a secure Service Account. No identifying contact details are stored.
