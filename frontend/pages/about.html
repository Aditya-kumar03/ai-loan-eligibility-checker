How It Works & Underwriting Logic

A deep dive into the financial formulas and rules governing loan underwriting in India.

1. Equated Monthly Installment (EMI)

In Indian retail banking, loans are structured using a reducing-balance amortization schedule. Interest is calculated on the remaining principal balance rather than the initial borrowed amount: 

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ - 1)

2. Debt-to-Income & FOIR

The Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR) measures the portion of net monthly income consumed by loan repayments and fixed liabilities. Under RBI lending norms, banks generally enforce a 45%–55% cap on post-loan FOIR.

3. Estimated Financial Eligibility Score (1000 Pts)

Our 1000-point transparent scoring engine evaluates Credit Bureau History (350 pts), Post-Loan DTI (250 pts), Income Stability (150 pts), Revolving Utilization (150 pts), and Cash Surplus (100 pts).
